A small studio, a lot of patience

Loop & Bloom started at a kitchen table with one hook and leftover yarn. Every keychain, bouquet and paranda you see here is still made the same way — one loop, one row, one piece at a time, checked by hand before it reaches you.

We keep runs small on purpose, so colors, sizes and little custom touches can always be worked out with you directly.
